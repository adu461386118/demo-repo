# Privacy Policy for Evelyn's Puzzle Game

Effective date: May 8, 2026

Evelyn's Puzzle Game does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data.

Gameplay progress, coins, stamina, language settings, achievements, gallery status, and collection progress are stored locally on the player's device. The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies.

Optional in-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Evelyn's Puzzle Game does not receive or store payment card details.

The app includes artwork and image assets used for puzzle gameplay. Public source and license information is kept in the app where applicable.

For support questions, see the support page for this app.
